The Babel fish is small, yellow, leech-like, and probably the oddest thing in the universe. It is also one of the most useful, which is saying something in a universe that contains tea and improbability drives.
<!-- /excerpt -->

The Babel fish solves the problem of interstellar communication by feeding on brain-wave energy received not from its own carrier but from those around it. It then excretes energy with a telepathic matrix formed by combining the conscious thought frequencies with nerve signals picked up from the speech centers of the brain. This is, by any standard, a very strange career.

## How It Works in Practice

- **Small:** The Babel fish is roughly the size of a small sardine, which is unfortunate because it lives in your ear.
- **Yellow:** Its color is bright yellow. If yours is not yellow, you may have a different fish in your ear. Seek help.
- **Leech**-li:ke This is not a value judgment. It is simply descriptive.
- **Telepathic** matr:ix The fish reads the brain waves of the speaker and translates the meaning directly into your mind.
- **Symbiotic:** You cannot survive without it. It cannot survive without you. It is a very one-sided symbiosis, but nobody consults the fish.

> [!TIP]
> If you begin hearing voices in languages you do not understand, the Babel fish may be on holiday. Wait a moment. If the voices persist, run.

## The Philosophical Problem

The Babel fish has been used to argue that God does not exist. The argument is as follows: a truly benevolent deity would not create a creature that proves useful precisely because it demonstrates that the universe is chaotic and random. The counter-argument is that a truly benevolent deity would also create tea. Both arguments are still under review.

> The Babel fish is the single most useful creature in the known universe. It is also the creature most likely to make you realize everyone is saying something embarrassing.

## Conclusion

Insert the Babel fish gently into your ear. Do not panic if it wriggles. That is a sign that it is working, or that it is not working, or that it is a fish and that is simply what fish do.
