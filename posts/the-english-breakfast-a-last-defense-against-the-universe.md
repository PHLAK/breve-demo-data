<!-- excerpt -->
There are many things I miss about Earth. High on the list, just below tea and a functioning door handle, is the full English breakfast. It is the only meal I have found that can make the universe feel, however briefly, like a sensible place.
<!-- /excerpt -->

[[TOC]]

The full English breakfast is not a meal. It is a statement. It says: "I am awake, I am prepared, and I have decided to face the day with a plate of beans, a sausage, and a small egg-shaped amount of hope." This is a powerful position from which to begin almost anything, up to and including the end of the world.

## The Essential Components

A proper breakfast can be divided into the following food groups. Please do not tell me there are officially five food groups. I have had a very trying week.

| Component | Required? | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bacon | Yes | Structural integrity. |
| Eggs | Yes | The sunniest part of the plate. |
| Sausage | Yes | Moral ballast. |
| Baked beans | Yes | Controversial, but essential. |
| Toast | Yes | For transferring beans. |
| Mushrooms | Negotiable | A bit of woodland. |
| Black pudding | Strongly advised | Do not ask what it is. Enjoy it. |
| Tea | Mandatory | Without this, the whole thing collapses. |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Never attempt an English breakfast with coffee. The bacon will know. The bacon will judge you.

## Why the Galaxy Gets It Wrong

The rest of the galaxy has many fine foods. I have been offered something that claimed to be a "full English" on three separate planets. Each time, the beans were the wrong color, the bacon was a kind of blue, and the toast had achieved a level of sentience I was not comfortable with. It was not an English breakfast. It was a misunderstanding.

> A proper breakfast is one of the things that made Earth worth not bulldozing. I am aware the bulldozers disagreed.

## Conclusion

If you are ever stranded in space, far from home, and someone offers you a full English breakfast, accept cautiously. Inspect the beans. Insist on tea. If they offer you a Pan-Galactic Gargle Blaster instead, you have accidentally sat down at Ford's table.
