Some people ask, "Zaphod, what makes you such a great President of the Galaxy?" I usually smile, adjust my shades, and say, "Everything." But because the *Guide* demands rigor, I have prepared a fully objective comparison.
<!-- /excerpt -->

## On Style

A president must look presidential. This means never wearing more than one hat at a time unless it is a very good party. It also means sunglasses, indoors, in all weather conditions. Leadership is 90% optics and 10% remembering where you parked your ship.

## Conclusion

I am the greatest. If you disagree, please submit your complaint in writing to the *Heart of Gold*'s complaints hatch, which opens directly into space. I'm very popular there too.
