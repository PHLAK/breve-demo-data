<!-- excerpt -->
There is an art to making a hasty retreat. Anyone can run. A true professional runs with posture, confidence, and a sense of showmanship that suggests the entire disaster was part of the plan.
<!-- /excerpt -->

When the authorities, creditors, or the entire population of a small planet are closing in, you have two choices: panic or pose. I always choose pose. Panic makes you sweat. Pose makes you memorable. A good escape is the only advertisement you will ever need.

## Dress for the Exit

| Scenario | Recommended Attire | Accessories |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Imperial fleet in pursuit | Velvet robe, no shoes | Sunglasses, towel |
| Angry former business partner | Formal tunic, half-buttoned | Two heads, confident smirk |
| Planetary destruction | Leather jacket, open collar | Stolen spaceship keys |
| Surprise press conference | Whatever you are already wearing | Microphone, evasive answer |
| Alien contract dispute | Naked is traditional, but risky | A lawyer and a fast ship |

> [!NOTE]
> The towel is not technically an accessory. It is essential equipment. Anybody who questions this is not worth fleeing with.

## The Three Rules of Flight

1. Never look back. It slows you down and ruins your silhouette.
2. Never apologize. Apologies imply fault, and fault is a legal concept.
3. Always smile. The camera is everywhere, and you have an image to maintain.

> I have been chased out of more galaxies than most people have visited. Every time, I looked incredible. Every time.

## Conclusion

You do not have to be the fastest person in the room. You only have to be the fastest person who looks good on the security footage. Priorities matter.
