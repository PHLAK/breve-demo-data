<!-- excerpt -->
The Restaurant at the End of the Universe is a solid four-star experience, assuming you enjoy the existential certainty of dining while the cosmos collapses around you. Reservations are strongly recommended.
<!-- /excerpt -->

Milliways is not a place you find. It is a place you find yourself at, usually after an improbability jump, a few panicked screams, and a complimentary dry martini. The atmosphere is formal but relaxed, the decor is tastefully apocalyptic, and the view is, quite literally, to die for.

## What to Wear

Milliways has a famously relaxed dress code, but as a rule, one should avoid looking like a recently planetless refugee. Towels are acceptable as accessories; they are not, however, acceptable as trousers.

> Dining at the end of all things gives you a certain perspective. Mostly it makes you want to order another drink before the stars go out.

## Getting There

The restaurant is situated at the end of time and matter, which is inconvenient but not impossible. Most patrons arrive via the *Heart of Gold* or a passing party ship. If neither is available, you may have to wait until the universe ends naturally, which is considered poor etiquette.
