The sunset is one of the galaxy's most overrated daily phenomena. It is also one of the most under-regulated. I have prepared a short guide to appreciating it properly, in the Vogon manner, which is the only manner worth mentioning.
<!-- /excerpt -->

Many people look at a sunset and think, "That is nice." This is lazy. A sunset is not nice. A sunset is a bureaucratic event in which the local star completes its daily paperwork and signs off in several wavelengths of light. It deserves respect. It deserves forms.

## The Proper Procedure

1. Observe the sunset. Do not merely look at it. Observation implies paperwork.
2. Record the color. Use a standardized color palette. "Pink" is not acceptable.
3. Estimate the rate of descent. Use a stopwatch. If you do not have a stopwatch, stamp your foot rhythmically.
4. Compose a poem. The poem should be no shorter than three verses and no longer than the patience of your audience.
5. Submit the poem for approval. It will be approved. You are a Vogon.
6. Watch the sunset set. If it does not set, file a complaint.

> [!CAUTION]
> Never enjoy a sunset without proper documentation. An undocumented sunset is a security risk. A sunset enjoyed by non-Vogons is a cultural tragedy.

## Ode to the Setting Star

> O ball of gas, you sink away,
> Into the west, or so they say.
> Your colors blaze, your light grows thin,
> A sight that fills my several chins.
>
> You vanish now, the night grows cold,
> Another form, another fold.
> I will remember you, I think,
> Upon the next official ink.

## Conclusion

A sunset is not a feeling. It is a process. A process is not a feeling either. This is the Vogon way, and the Vogon way is the correct way because we have the paperwork to prove it.
