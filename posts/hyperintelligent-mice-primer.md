Most researchers assume that mice are small furry test subjects running through mazes. This is a common misconception. The mice are running the maze. The maze is us. Here is what to do about it.
<!-- /excerpt -->

If you have recently discovered that your mouse is not, in fact, a mouse, do not panic. This is a routine revelation for anyone traveling on the *Heart of Gold*. The important thing is to understand the nature of the being you are dealing with, and to stop doing experiments on it immediately.

## Glossary of Pan-Dimensional Mouse Terms

 - **Hyper-intelligent:** A relative term. From their perspective, most humans are about as clever as a warm pair of socks.
 - **Pan-dimensional:** Existing in more dimensions than are strictly polite at dinner parties.
 - **Squeak:** Not a vocalization. A compressed form of higher mathematics that your brain is too simple to unpack.
 - **Nibbling:** A method of calibrating local probability fields. Do not interrupt.

> [!NOTE]
> If your mouse looks at you with a small, dark, knowing eye, it is almost certainly making a note about your performance. Try not to take it personally.

## A Simple Diagnostic Script

If you suspect your mouse is running the simulation, the following check may help confirm your hypothesis:

```javascript
function analyzeMouse(subject) {
    if (subject.isRunningTheExperiment) {
        return "You are the subject."
    }
    if (subject.squeaksIncludeTopology) {
        return "Apologize immediately."
    }
    if (subject.wantsCheese) {
        return "A decoy. Provide the cheese anyway."
    }
    return "Probably an ordinary mouse. Probably."
}

console.log(analyzeMouse(myMouse));
```

## What Not to Do

Do not ask them to build a planet. They already did, and the results were mixed.[^1]

[^1]: The original Earth was a collaborative project with the dolphins, which is why both species were so deeply unimpressed with us. The second attempt is best regarded as a beta.

## Conclusion

Treat your mouse with respect, provide a comfortable wheel, and never assume you are the cleverest mammal in the room. The odds are not in your favor.
